Happy 2026, everyone! Last year, we ramped up our games to add Second [Sin Brewing Company](https://www.facebook.com/secondsinbeer) to our monthly lineup, joining [Broken Goblet Brewing](https://www.facebook.com/BrokenGoblet). Unfortunately, we won't be returning to Second Sin Brewing - **Newtown** due to low player turnout (Note that this is **ONLY** the Newtown location, **NOT** Bristol!). But that means we can put more focus on Second Sin - Bristol and BGob, and potentially expand to a third location. We may already be in contact with another local brewery, so make sure to follow [our Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/drink20Philly/) for up-to-date info (yes, we post most on Facebook than here)!

2025 was a great year for games. We averaged about 18-22 players at The Mixing Room at Broken Goblet, we held our first non-D&D game at Second Sin, and we made some awesome friends at each location. We're hoping that 2026 is even better! Our goals are to grow a little and to try some new games at all of our events.

We know D&D is _the_ TTRPG right now, but we have a collection of amazing games that we think you'll love just as much, and maybe you’ll your new favorite. So, don't be surprised if 2026 brings games like Monster of the Week or Daggerheart to our regular events. Make a resolution to try something new, you may be surprised how much you like these systems!

Lastly, we've been asked if we’ll run non-TTRPG games. While we don’t have any board/card game nights planned, we’re open to the idea. Our specialty is TTRPGs, but we know that's not everyone's favorite flavor, and we try to be welcoming to all members of the analog gaming community.

Thank you to our players for showing up with creativity and energizarme, and thank you to the breweries for allowing us to run our games at your locations. You all rock! Please share this, tell your friends, and bring someone new to the next event!

Happy 2026!

Cheers,
