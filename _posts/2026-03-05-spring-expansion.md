Rejoice, fellow adventurers! We have finally made it through the worst of winter, and spring is right around the corner. Flowers are blooming, trees are budding, and Drink20 is expanding! This month (March 2026), we will be holding our first event at [Aristaeus Brewing](https://aristaeusbrewing.com/) in Langhorne, PA. They will be the third brewery to host us, and we are excited to run games at a location voted Bucks County’s Best Brewery three years in a row!

![Aristaeus Logo](/assets/img/breweries/aristaeus-light-web.png)

Our first event at Aristaeus will be on Wednesday, March 25, 2026, from 6:30 to 9:30 pm. More information can be found on [the Facebook event page](https://www.facebook.com/share/14VbkRhx6X2/). We hope to continue at Aristaeus every Fourth Wednesday of the month.

### Our other March events are:

Second Sin Bristol on Thursday, March 12, 2026, from 6-9 pm. More info can be found on that [Facebook Event Page](https://www.facebook.com/share/14VbkRhx6X2/)

Broken Goblet (aka Goblets and Goblins) on Wednesday, March 18, 2026, from 6-9:30 pm. More info can be found [on Facebook as well](https://www.facebook.com/share/1AWtYWmup6/)

Our events are always free to play, and open to all players 18+ years of age. We supply everything players need, including dice, minis, and character sheets, though players are always welcome to bring their own supplies or characters (subject to DM approval).

The best way to know the latest Drink20 news and info is to follow us on [Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/drink20Philly)!

If you have any questions, comments, or concerns, fill out our [contact form](/contact).

We hope to roll some dice with you soon!
