#### What is the best way to find out about Drink20 events? <a id="faq_how_to_get_info"></a>

We use Facebook to coordinate events. The reaosn for this is becaue all of the locations that we work with use Facebook for their events and is their primary social media platform for sharing with its customers. We typically create the event with [our account](https://www.facebook.com/drink20Philly) and add the brewery as a co-host.

#### Do I need to sign up for an event to join a game? <a id="faq_signups"></a>

Signups are *not required* to join in any Drink20 event. However, if you are able to RSVP to the Facebook event page, that really helps with planning for us and our hosts. 

#### How can I sign up to run a game? <a id="#faq_signup_to_run"></a>

You can [contact us](/contact) and let us know what you're interested in running and what locations you are interested in running out of. We are not always in need of DMs; however, we like having a list of folks that are interested for future events and for backups.

#### Are there any age restrictions for events? <a id="faq_age_restrictions"></a>

Anyone under 21 can legally come to a brewery as long as their rules allow this (see the Respect the Venue section of our [Code of Conduct](/CoC)). Players *under 18* can sit near you and play their own game, provided you supervise them at all times; however, *they are not allowed to play D&D at our tables*. You assume all liability for your children, and we take no responsibility for any language or adult-oriented content that your children may overhear.

Additionally, Pennsylvania law dictates that anyone under 21 must be accompanied by an adult at all times while at a brewery. Additionally, Pennsylvania law dictates that anyone working directly with minors must have a series of state clearances. Unfortunately, none of our DMs have these clearances, so they cannot _legally_ run a game for minors.

If you would like a D&D event where children can play, Drink20 recommends looking into [Queen and Rook in Philadelphia](https://queenandrookcafe.com/philadelphia-queen-village-queen-and-rook-game-cafe-youth-programs). They run events specifically geared towards tweens and teens.

#### I want to have Drink20 run a game at my brewery/business, how can I get that started? <a id="faq_i_want_to_run"></a>

The best way to start is to [contact us](/contact) and let us know what you're interested in us running. We run *tabletop RPGs (TTRPGs)*; things like Dungeons and Dragons, Masks, Monster of the Week, and Daggerheart. We do not run board games (Ticket to Ride, Catan, Scrabble, etc.) or card games (Magic, poker, etc.). If you want to have people play non-TTRPG games during an event that we are running, that is totally fine, but we will not be able to manage those games.
